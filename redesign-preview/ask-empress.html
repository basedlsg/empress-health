Questions

About Ask Empress

How do I use Ask Empress?

Type a question above, or choose one of the suggested questions. Each answer is based on your current question. Include the relevant details each time, and open the retrieved passages to review the source material.

Am I talking to AI? Who checks what it tells me?

Yes — Ask Empress is AI. Its answers use the Empress knowledge library, and the retrieved passages appear beneath each answer. Those passages explain the material used; they do not independently verify every generated statement. Empress provides education and guidance, and cannot diagnose you or prescribe treatment.

Is Ask Empress a doctor?

No. Ask Empress provides education and guidance. It doesn't diagnose conditions or prescribe treatment, and it will suggest seeing a clinician when your question needs one.
