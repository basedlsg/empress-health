Why do I keep forgetting words mid-sentence? I'm 46 and it's scaring me.
That's frightening to experience — and it's very common in perimenopause. Shifting estrogen affects the brain areas involved in word retrieval, and broken sleep makes it worse.
For most women it improves after the transition. It's worth noting when it happens and how you slept the night before. Want me to add that to your tracker?
If memory changes are sudden, getting worse quickly, or come with other new symptoms, Empress will suggest seeing a clinician promptly.
