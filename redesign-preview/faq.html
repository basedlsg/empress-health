Getting started

What is Empress Health.ai?

Empress Health.ai is a platform built specifically for women 35+ navigating perimenopause and menopause. It brings together evidence-based guidance, Ask Empress, symptom tracking, expert input and a curated marketplace — all in one place. Think of us as the system your doctor never had time to build for you.

Who is Empress Health.ai for?

Any woman 35+ who is in perimenopause, menopause, or post-menopause — or who suspects she might be. If you've been told your labs are “normal” but you feel anything but, if you've been dismissed, or if you're simply trying to get ahead of what's coming, Empress was built for you.

I'm still getting periods. Is this for me?

Very likely, yes. Perimenopause often starts in your late thirties or forties while cycles are still regular. It's the stage most often missed — and one Empress was built for.

How is Empress different from my regular doctor?

Your doctor diagnoses and treats, and Empress doesn't replace that. What Empress adds is everything in between appointments: a place to understand your symptoms, track what's changing, ask questions at any hour, and walk into your next visit prepared.

Is Empress medical care? Can it prescribe hormone therapy?

No. Empress provides education, guidance and wellness tools built with experts. It doesn't diagnose conditions or prescribe medication. It helps you understand your options and prepare for the conversation with your own clinician.

Your Consult Report

What is the Consult Report?

A personalized, printable summary built from your assessment and tracking — your symptom picture, likely stage, what you've tried and questions to ask your clinician. It's included with every membership, so there's nothing extra to buy. See what's included.

How is the Consult Report built?

From your full Health Intelligence assessment: 120 health markers across 10 body systems, no blood draws, about 15 minutes. It's open to members. Please set aside time to finish it in one sitting. The free 3-minute assessment gives you a shorter snapshot before you join.

Is the report a diagnosis?

No. It organizes what you've told us, with evidence-based guidance. Diagnosis and treatment decisions belong with your clinician — the report is designed to make that conversation more productive.

Ask Empress

What is Ask Empress?

A 24/7 companion for perimenopause and menopause questions, grounded in peer-reviewed research and shaped by our experts. It's honest about its limits and tells you when a question needs a clinician.
