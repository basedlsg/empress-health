What it looks at

120 health markers, across 10 body systems

Hormonal change rarely stays in one place. The assessment asks about each system in turn, so patterns that look unrelated on their own can be seen together.

✦

Hormonal & cycle

Where you are in the transition, and how your cycle has changed.

◷

Sleep

Falling asleep, staying asleep, night sweats and early waking.

❋

Vasomotor

Hot flashes, chills, flushing and how often they interrupt your day.

✧

Mood & stress

Irritability, low mood, anxiety and how you're coping week to week.

◍

Cognition

Focus, memory, word-finding and the brain fog nobody warned you about.

♡

Heart & metabolic

Energy, weight changes, appetite and known risk factors in your history.

◆

Bone, joints & muscle

Aches, stiffness, strength and activity you've had to give up.

❉

Skin, hair & nails

Dryness, thinning, texture changes and what you've already tried.

✽

Urogenital & sexual health

Dryness, discomfort, bladder changes and libido — asked plainly, without embarrassment.

◎

Digestive & general

Gut changes, headaches, medications, supplements and family history.

No blood draws — every answer comes from you. Nothing here is a diagnosis; it's a structured picture you and your clinician can work from.
